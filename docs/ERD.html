Fitness Planner · Draft ERD

Milestone 1 planning only. PK = primary key; FK = foreign key; ? = nullable. Relationship labels give endpoint cardinalities.

  • Users 1 → 0..many Plans and Sessions.
  • Plans 1 → 0..many Plan Exercises; Exercises 1 → 0..many Plan Exercises.
  • A Session optionally references one Plan; a Plan can have many Sessions.
  • Sessions 1 → 0..many Logs; Exercises 1 → 0..many Logs.

See ERD.md for constraints, ownership and deletion policies.
